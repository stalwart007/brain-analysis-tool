Membership is voluntary, compensated, and revocable at any time.

What we collect — the full list

  • Interaction rhythms only: scroll speed, pauses, click patterns, and time spent in labeled page areas, on participating partner sites only.
  • Never collected: page text you read, anything you type, passwords, browsing outside partner sites, or anything identifying you.
  • Your data is stored under a pseudonymous panel ID and used to build aggregate, synthetic research personas — never an individual profile of you.
  • Revoking below permanently deletes every session ever linked to your panel ID. No archive, no grace period.

By accepting, you consent to the collection described above.
